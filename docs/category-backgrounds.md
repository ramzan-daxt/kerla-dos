# Category background assets

`public/backgrounds/chechen-ornament-v1.png` is a transparent decorative tile
generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-09-07. It is an illustrative
interpretation, not a sourced reproduction of a historical ornament.

Prompt: Actual standalone raster seamless square repeating background tile for
a children's educational app. Very sparse fine monochrome muted sage green
thin-outline doodles inspired by Chechen culture: recognizable slender Vainakh
stone towers with pyramidal stepped roofs, simple Caucasus mountain ridges,
small restrained interlaced ornamental curls, and tiny leafy sprigs. Small
separated motifs, at least 85% visually empty space, flat delicate line art,
no shading or fills. Transparent background preferred. No lettering, flags,
mosque, people, UI, frame, border, or watermark.

Category-specific motifs reuse existing category icons, including the approved
national-food icon. Both decorative layers are noninteractive, subdued and
outside the white cards. The change is limited to category learning/game pages;
story illustrations and the main page are unchanged.

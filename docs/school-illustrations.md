the existing school words. Category icon, spelling, classes, translations and
recordings stay unchanged. Final assets: `public/illustrations/school/*-v1.png`.

Shared prompt: square genuinely transparent child vocabulary illustration;
softly dimensional hand-painted gouache, clean outlines, natural colors;
whole subject centered with 8–10% clear margins; no text, logos or watermark.

pencil without glow; blue ballpoint pen; male teacher with book and small board;
schoolboy with backpack and notebook (not a graduate); school building;
cutaway classroom with desks and board; school desk and chair; green chalkboard;
wooden ruler with ticks; rounded-tip child scissors; teal school backpack;

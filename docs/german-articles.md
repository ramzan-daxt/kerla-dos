# German articles on vocabulary cards

Existing German noun translations now include the definite article in the
nominative. Articles are part of the shared translation data, not derived from
Chechen class badges. German noun capitalization and existing singular/plural
choices are preserved. Colors, numerals and adverbs are unchanged.

Special cases: Утро = der Morgen, Завтра = Morgen (adverb); Ключ = der Schlüssel
(singular, updated by owner); Двоюродный брат или сестра = der Cousin oder die Cousine;
Воздух; атмосфера = die Luft; die Atmosphäre.

Variant genders checked against Duden:
- [Paprika](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Paprika_Gemuese): vegetable permits
  feminine and masculine; the cards use die Paprika.
- [Ziesel](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Ziesel): der or das; the cards use
  der Ziesel (das is especially Austrian).

Do not claim all translations were newly dictionary-verified: this change adds
articles to existing translations. Tests cover every current noun card and
alphabet example, and exclude non-nouns explicitly.
